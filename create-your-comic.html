Adventure 1 · Create Your Comic Available now

Twelve weeks. Every child publishes their own comic.

Each child invents an original hero and publishes a printed comic of 4 to 8 pages, learning eight AI skills along the way. The child is the author; AI is their illustrator and editor.

See the twelve weeks Book a demo

  2. Weeks 4–6

  3. Week 7 · Storyboard Pages, panels, dialogue. On paper. No AI this week.

Week by week

Five phases, one mission a week.

Meet your hero, build the world and the story, storyboard, make the pages, publish. Every week ends with something concrete saved on the platform.

  1. 01
    Welcome to the Comic StudioRead a sample comic and learn panels, speech bubbles and sound effects. Watch the same AI give a vague result and a precise one. Write three hero ideas on paper.AI skill · the more precise you are, the better the result
    3 hero ideas
  2. 02
    Design your heroPick one idea, draw the hero on paper, then describe them: look, personality, superpower, one weakness. Photograph the sketch, make four versions, pick and refine one.AI skill · prompting with details: who, what they look like, style
    Hero, version 1
  3. 03
    Lock your heroBuild the character sheet: front view, three expressions, outfit, name. Choose the comic style: manga, cartoon, superhero or local heritage.AI skill · consistency: reusing a reference so AI draws the same character every time
    Character sheet
  4. 04
    Friends and enemiesCreate a sidekick (8–9) or a sidekick and a villain (10–11). AI suggests five ideas; the child picks, mixes or rejects them.AI skill · AI as a brainstorm partner: AI suggests, you decide
    1–2 more characters
  5. 05
    Build the worldChoose where the story happens: a souq, the desert, a pearl-diving city under the sea, space, school. Make two or three backgrounds.AI skill · describing places: mood, light, time of day
    2–3 setting cards
  6. 06
    Write the storyWrite it on the five-line story spine: Once upon a time, Every day, Until one day, Because of that, Until finally. AI asks feedback questions and never rewrites.AI skill · AI as an editor, not a ghostwriter
    Written story
  7. 07
    StoryboardOn paper: split the story into pages and panels, sketch each panel with stick figures, write the dialogue.AI skill · planning before generating (no AI this week)
    Storyboard + script
  8. 08
    Make the pages (1)Make the first pages panel by panel: character, action, place and camera angle.AI skill · building a full prompt from parts
    First 2 pages
  9. 09
    Make the pages (2)Carry on with the pages, and play the AI mistake hunt: extra fingers, wrong outfits, gibberish text. Then fix them.AI skill · AI makes mistakes; always check its work
    All panels drafted
  10. 10
    Words and coverAdd speech bubbles, narration boxes and sound effects, typed by the children themselves. Design the cover with the title and the author’s name.AI skill · knowing what AI is bad at: writing text inside pictures
    Draft + cover
  11. 11
    Review and polishSwap comics with a partner: two stars and a wish. Fix the comic. Fill in the “How I used AI” page: what I made, and what AI made.AI skill · honesty and ownership: who is the artist?
    Final comic
  12. 12
    Comic ConPrint, present the comic in one minute to the group and parents, awards. Teaser: in Adventure 2, your hero comes to life.AI skill · presenting your work and explaining how you used AI
    Published comic

How a session runs

One hour a week, same rhythm every time. The teacher follows the script; Candide runs the AI.

Warm-up · 5 min
Mini-lesson · 10 min
Studio time · 35 min hands-on
Share & save · 10 min
  1. Warm-up Real or AI? Look closely at the hands.
    01 · Warm-up · 5 min

    Kids join and focus

    A “Real or AI?” picture or one quick AI fact, while children type the group code and tap their name.

  2. Week 2 · Design your hero Go to app.candideai.com/join K7QX4M 14 joined
    02 · Mini-lesson · 10 min

    The teacher plays the week’s lesson

    A short video and demo on the class screen, then the mission explained. The script tells them what to say.

  3. 03 · Studio time · 35 min

    Every child creates

    On paper or on the platform, with an AI helper that guides and never does the work for them. The teacher circulates.

  4. 04 · Share & save · 10 min

    Two or three kids show their work

    Everyone saves, the gallery goes up, and the teacher previews next week. Their work is waiting for them next time.

  • Rule 1 · You are the authorIdeas come from you; AI helps you make them.
  • Rule 2 · Three triesImprove your prompt, don’t spam the button. After three tries, change your words or ask a friend.
  • Paper and screenSketches, story spines and storyboards happen on paper, to keep it hands-on.
  • Saved every weekEach week ends with something concrete on their board, ready for the next one.

What children learn

Eight AI skills, learned by making something they’re proud of.

Week 1 starts with this: same AI, two prompts. Children see for themselves that the words they choose decide what they get, and that a wrong picture is something they can fix.

  • Precise words, better resultsAI follows instructions. Week 1.
  • Writing a detailed promptWho, what they look like, style. Weeks 2 and 8.
  • Keeping a character consistentReusing a reference so the hero looks the same. Week 3.
  • Brainstorming with AIAI suggests, you decide. Week 4.
  • AI as an editor, not a ghostwriterIt asks questions; you write. Week 6.
  • Spotting and fixing mistakesExtra fingers, wrong outfits, gibberish text. Weeks 9 and 10.
  • Creating original workYour own hero, not someone else’s. Weeks 2 and 11.
  • Being honest about AIWhat I made, and what AI made. Weeks 11 and 12.
