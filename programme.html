Eight weeks, one growing world

Nothing is thrown away between projects. The creature from week one is the hero of the story in week four and the star of the trailer in week seven.

What your child practises

Four habits that outlast any tool

The tools will keep changing. What we teach is the thinking around them, and children practise it in every session.

Express

Turn the picture in their head into words precise enough for someone else, or something else, to act on.

Experiment

Try it, change one thing, try again. Constraints turn it into a game.

Evaluate

Look hard at a result and decide whether it’s really what they meant.

Improve

Edit, cut and rework until the piece is theirs, then stand up and present it.
