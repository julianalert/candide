Week by week

Five phases, one mission a week.

Understand the market, design the toy, build the brand, sell it, Toy Fair. Every week ends with something concrete saved on the platform.

  1. 01
    What makes a hit toy?Study three famous kinds of toy: a collectible figure, trading cards, building bricks. Ask AI why they sell, then check its answers against what the class thinks.AI skill · AI as a research assistant that can be wrong
    Hit toy recipe
  2. 02
    Know your customerPick who the toy is for and interview three classmates with prepared questions. Compare their answers with an AI “pretend customer”.AI skill · real research versus AI guessing
    Customer card
  3. 03
    Design the toyChoose the type of toy (figure, plush, keychain, card) and sketch it on paper. Turn the comic hero into a toy photo with AI: three concepts, then pick one.AI skill · restyling the same character for a new use
    Toy design
  4. 04
    Make it a collectionDesign variants with new outfits and colours, and one rare secret edition. Talk about why mystery boxes and rare items make people spend more.AI skill · variations that keep the character, and how marketing uses surprise
    Collection sheet
  5. 05
    Build the prototypeTurn the design into a 3D model, turn it around, spot the problems and fix them. Then the teacher prints it, or the child folds the paper-toy version.AI skill · from 2D to 3D, and what AI gets wrong in 3D
    3D model + prototype
  6. 06
    Brand itAI suggests ten names; the child picks or invents one and checks it isn’t already a real brand. Then a logo, a slogan and brand colours.AI skill · brainstorming with AI and checking originality
    Brand card
  7. 07
    Design the boxFront, back and sides: the toy and its name, the hero’s story from the comic, the age label. Children write the text, AI suggests improvements, then they print and build the box.AI skill · writing persuasive text yourself, with AI as a reviewer
    Toy box
  8. 08
    Price itWork out what the toy costs to make, set a price and calculate the profit. AI does the same maths; children check it with a calculator.AI skill · AI can make maths mistakes: always check the numbers
    Price card
  9. 09
    Make the adA poster (ages 8–9) or a 15 to 30 second video using their Term 2 film skills (ages 10–11). Talk about honest and misleading claims.AI skill · persuasion and honesty in advertising
    Ad
  10. 10
    Build the pitchWrite the pitch in five steps: the toy, who it’s for, why they’ll love it, the price, the ask. Make three to five slides.AI skill · turning a story into a short, clear message
    Pitch + slides
  11. 11
    Practise with an AI coachRehearse in pairs, then face the AI judge, which asks three typical questions. Time the pitch and improve it.AI skill · AI as a practice coach, and acting on feedback
    Final pitch
  12. 12
    Toy Fair and Pitch DaySet up a stand, pitch to the judges, parents invest play tokens, awards, and take home the year portfolio.AI skill · presenting your work and how AI helped
    Year portfolio

How a session runs

One hour a week, same rhythm every time. The teacher follows the script; Candide runs the AI.

Real-world tasks keep it hands-on: interviewing classmates, building a paper box, doing the maths.

What children learn

Eight AI skills, learned by building a toy business.

Week 2 starts with this: ask AI who will buy the toy, then ask three classmates. Children see for themselves that AI guesses and people know.

  • Researching with AI, then checkingAI can be wrong about facts. Week 1.
  • Asking real peopleAI guesses; people know. Week 2.
  • Restyling a characterFrom comic hero to toy. Weeks 3 and 4.
  • From 2D to 3DAnd what AI gets wrong in 3D. Week 5.
  • Checking originalityTen names from AI, then check it isn’t a real brand. Week 6.
  • Checking AI’s numbersEvery price checked with a calculator. Week 8.
  • Advertising honestlySpotting marketing tricks, and not using them. Weeks 4 and 9.
  • Practising with an AI coachThree judge questions, then a better pitch. Week 11.

Safety

Products, money and ads, handled with care.

Every rule from Terms 1 and 2 still applies. Toys, money, ads and a pitch coach add these.

  • No real moneyParents invest play tokens. Nothing is sold on the platform.
  • No copying real brandsOriginal toys and names only; well-known toy brands are blocked.
  • Honest advertisingAds can’t make false claims, and week 9 teaches why.
  • A coach that sticks to the pitchIt only asks about the toy and the business, and the teacher sees every conversation.

Week 12 · Toy Fair & Pitch Day

The whole year, on one stand.

Each child runs a stand and pitches to judges, and parents see a comic, a film and a toy made by their child. Candide supplies the stand signs, tokens or QR voting, judges’ cards, awards, certificates and the invitation.

  • A stand per childThe prototype, the box, the collection sheet, and the ad playing on a tablet.
  • A judges’ panelThe principal, a teacher, and a parent who runs a business.
  • Parents investFive play tokens each; the most-invested toy wins People’s Choice.
  • The year portfolioThe printed comic, a QR code to the film, the toy box and the certificate.
