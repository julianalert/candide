Safety by design

Children never touch an open AI model.

Every mission runs inside rules the child cannot change, with a check on the way in and another on the way out. Safety is built into the platform, not left to the teacher.

  1. The child writesOr speaks, with the microphone button.
  2. Checked on the way inBefore anything reaches the AI. Off-mission messages get one gentle line.
  3. The AI helper answersIn the week’s role, within a per-child budget.

    Fixed safety layer: no personal information, no real people, no copyrighted characters, nothing that doesn’t belong at school.

  4. Checked on the way outEvery reply, before the child sees it. Pictures come from a rewritten, safe prompt, modest by default.
  5. The child reads, and keeps goingThe AI stays a tool in their hands, not a game.
  • No child accountsA group code and their first name on the group’s list. No email, no password, no selfies.
  • Nothing leaves the groupNo chat between children, no public sharing. Gallery links are private.
  • Teacher in controlThe teacher sees every child’s work and can end the session at any time.
  • Hosted in the EUNever used to train AI models. Transcripts deleted after 90 days.
