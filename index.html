Kids shouldn’t just use AI. They should create with it.

Candide is an after-school AI academy for children aged 8 to 12. In small groups, they invent a creature, write its adventure and premiere its trailer. Along the way they learn to shape an idea, test it, judge the result and make it better.

The Candide method

AI is the tool. Creativity is the curriculum.

A beautiful image doesn’t prove a child learned anything. So every Candide project ends the same way: the child explains their work. By the end of term, they can answer four questions about anything they’ve made.

  1. 1

    What was my own idea?

    Every project starts away from the screen, with a sketch, a few sentences or a conversation. The idea is theirs before any AI is involved.

  2. 2

    What did the AI contribute?

    Children learn to tell their own choices apart from what the tool produced, and to say so honestly.

  3. 3

    What went wrong?

    First results are rarely right. We treat the misses as the interesting part: why didn’t it come out the way you pictured it?

  4. 4

    What did I change, and why?

    They revise, compare versions and justify the one they keep. That habit is what we’re really teaching.

Run by adults.

Children use AI at Candide the way they’d use a kiln or a science lab: with the right equipment and an adult close by.

Questions

What parents ask first.

Something we haven’t covered? Add it to the last field when you join the founding list and we’ll answer it.

Does my child need experience with AI or coding?

No. There’s no coding, and we start from zero with the AI tools. What helps is being able to read instructions, explain a choice and work with a little independence, which is why the founding term is for ages 8 to 12.

Is this just more screen time?

About 35 of the 90 minutes are hands-on building with AI tools. The rest is spent discussing examples, planning on paper, watching a short demonstration and presenting work to the group. See how a session runs.

Which AI tools will my child use?

Established tools, each one checked for age suitability and access. Enrolled families receive the list with the consent form before the term starts.

When and where does it run?

In person in Doha, once a week for eight weeks. We’re confirming the venue and dates now, and families on the founding list will hear first.

How much does it cost?

We’ll share the fee for the founding term with the founding list, together with the dates and venue. Joining the list is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.

My child is 6 or 7. Can they join?

Not this term. A programme for younger children, with shorter sessions and more guided activities, is planned for later. Join the founding list, choose that age, and we’ll tell you when it opens.

What does my child take home at the end?

A portfolio with their creature, their illustrated story and their trailer, including the early versions and what they changed along the way.
