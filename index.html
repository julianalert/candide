Your questions

Is Candide right for your school?

Does the teacher who runs the sessions need any AI experience?

No. Every week’s mission comes with a script on the class screen: the warm-up, the short video and demo, how to explain the mission, what to look for during studio time, and how to run share and save. The platform handles the AI, and the teacher learns alongside the kids.

How does it fit our activities programme?

It runs like your other activities: one hour a week for a term, twelve sessions, during an activity slot or after school. For a ten-week term there is a shorter version that merges four of the weeks. It sits outside your official curriculum, so there’s nothing to change in the timetable.

Which ages is it for?

Ages 8 to 11, in two tracks. Ages 8–9 make a 4-page comic with a hero and a sidekick, using a guided prompt builder and read-aloud. Ages 10–11 make 6 to 8 pages with a villain and a plot twist, and write their prompts freely.

What happens at the end of term?

Comic Con. Each child presents their comic in one minute to the group and to parents, gets a printed A5 copy and a “Published Author & AI Creator” certificate, and the group’s comics become an anthology for your library. It’s the moment parents remember, and the reason groups fill again next term.

Do children need accounts or email addresses?

No. The teacher types the children’s first names once. Each week, children type the group code and tap their name, and their work from last week is there. No email, no password, no date of birth. They only ever upload photos of their paper sketches, never of themselves.

What can the AI see and say?

Only what the child types during the session, inside a fixed safety layer and the week’s own instructions. Each message is checked before it reaches the AI and each reply is checked before the child sees it. The AI never asks for personal information, never draws real people or characters that belong to someone else, and redirects anything off-mission.

Which AI models do you use?

Claude, by Anthropic, for the conversation and the safety checks, and a separate image model with a safety tier for pictures. Children’s data is not used to train any model.

Where is the data hosted, and for how long?

In the EU. Transcripts are kept for 90 days for safety review, then deleted. Children’s creations stay until the school asks for them to be removed, which a school admin can do in one click.

What comes after Term 1?

Children keep the same hero all year. In Term 2 their hero comes to life in an animated film; in Term 3 they turn it into a toy and pitch it. Term 1 is available now; the next terms follow.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many groups you run each term. Book a demo and we’ll share the figure with a plan for your school.
